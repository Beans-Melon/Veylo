Protection without overstatement

A veil you choose
when to lift.

Veylo takes its name from the idea of a veil: a protective layer between your private information and everyday use. Created by ElctroZeus.

Read the security policy
  • Encrypted storage: AES-256-GCM with Argon2id key derivation.
  • Explicit access: manual reveal, copy and selected filling; automatic idle locking.
  • Recovery responsibility: keep your master passphrase, unlock secret and encrypted backups separately.
  • Clear boundaries: test software, not independently audited. Encryption does not make a compromised device trustworthy.

Before you begin

A few important details.

Will my phone, Windows app and Chrome extension automatically stay in sync?

No. These builds keep separate local vaults. Supported local sync requires pairing and a reviewed exchange. The planned Veylo Link hub is not yet released, and the current packages do not provide automatic three-way sync.

How do I move an existing Veylo vault to Windows?

Export a standard encrypted .vvlt backup from Veylo Android or Chrome. On a fresh Windows vault screen, choose Restore encrypted backup and enter the original credentials. Android NFC YubiKey-protected backups cannot be opened by the Windows build. Read the Windows guide.

Does every platform have the same features?

No. Biometrics, NFC YubiKey, camera OCR and Bluetooth keyboard Auto-Type are Android features. Website filling is provided by Chrome. The full feature reference explains the differences and lists requested features that are not implemented.

Is this a production-ready, independently audited password manager?

No. Automated tests and package checks have been performed, but independent auditing and broad physical-device validation have not. Begin with dummy data, test recovery, and retain your established password manager and backups.
